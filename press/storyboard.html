PDF Diva: demo video storyboard

40 seconds, no voice. Shot list and notes in video-script.md. “Audience screen” = the hall projection (booth) or the monitor (simulated booth).

10:00–0:04
Close-up

A hand puts a USB stick on the booth desk, next to the laptop.

Sooner or later, a speaker hands you a PDF.

20:04–0:09
Over the shoulder, laptop screen

Right-click the PDF → Open with → PDF Diva. The reader opens with its thumbnails. (Or drag the PDF onto the window.)

30:09–0:12
Close-up, hand and screen

Click Present.

40:12–0:18
Wide — the shot that explains the product

Through the booth window, the slide fills the audience screen; the laptop shows the speaker view in the foreground. Hold still.

The slide on the projector.

50:18–0:25
Close-up on the laptop, remote in the foreground

Two clicks on the remote: “Page 2 of 8”, “Page 3 of 8”; the timer runs. The remote must not hide the timer or the page count.

Next slide and timer for the speaker.

60:25–0:29
Medium, audience screen

Press B: the audience screen goes black. Press it again: the slide is back. One continuous shot (the drawing shows the two moments).

Black screen with one key.

70:29–0:36
Medium, laptop and audience screen in frame

Pull the video cable out at the laptop end. While it's out, the laptop shows the slide fullscreen. Plug it back in: the slide returns to the audience screen, the speaker view to the laptop, same page. Real time.

A cable comes loose. It carries on.

80:36–0:40
End card (made in the edit)

App icon, name, tagline, website. Dark background, holds at least 3 s.

Captions: Segoe UI Semibold, white on a dark translucent bar, lower third. Calm tone: describe what happens, no slogans.
