# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

PDF Diva is a free, open-source desktop app for presenting PDF files. It is built to work completely offline.

## What PDF Diva collects

Nothing. PDF Diva:

- has no accounts and no sign-in;
- has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or advertising;
- does not connect to the internet, not even to check for updates;
- does not send your PDF files, or any information about them, anywhere.

## What stays on your computer

PDF Diva saves a few settings on your computer so it remembers them next time: the monitor used for the speaker view and the theme (System, Light or Dark). They are stored in your Windows user profile and never leave your computer. Uninstalling the app removes them.

PDF Diva only opens the PDF files you choose. It does not copy, change or upload them. Passwords for protected PDFs are used only to open the file and are never saved.

## Links

The start screen has a link to the project page. If you click it, it opens in your web browser, and the website's own privacy policy applies.

## Where you download the app

PDF Diva itself collects nothing, but the place you download it from may collect data under its own policy:

- **Microsoft Store**: see the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
- **GitHub**: see the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. The full history is in the project repository.
